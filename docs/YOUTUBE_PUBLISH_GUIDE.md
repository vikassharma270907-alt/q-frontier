## 🚀 5. Step-by-Step Publishing Walkthrough in YouTube Studio

Follow these 6 simple steps to publish your video demo:

```
[1. studio.youtube.com] → [2. Upload Video] → [3. Upload Thumbnail] → [4. Paste Metadata] → [5. Visibility] → [6. Embed in Devpost]
```

### Step 1: Open YouTube Studio
1. Open your browser and navigate to **[https://studio.youtube.com/](https://studio.youtube.com/)**.
2. Sign in with your Google / Team account.

### Step 2: Upload the Master MP4 Video
1. In the top right corner, click **CREATE** (the video camera icon) $\rightarrow$ **Upload videos**.
2. Drag and drop or browse to select the master video file:
   ```text
   D:\Intermountain Quantum Competition\docs\Q_FRONTIER_DEMO_VIDEO.mp4
   ```

### Step 3: Upload the Custom Thumbnail
1. In the **Thumbnail** section, click **Upload thumbnail**.
2. Select the generated thumbnail file:
   ```text
   D:\Intermountain Quantum Competition\docs\YOUTUBE_THUMBNAIL.png
   ```

### Step 4: Fill In Details
1. **Title:** Paste Option A:  
   `Q-FRONTIER: Context-Aware Counterdiabatic Quantum Portfolio Optimization | IQC 2026 Demo (BYU)`
2. **Description:** Copy and paste the formatted text block from **Section 3** above.
3. **Audience:** Select *"No, it's not made for kids"*.
4. Click **Show more**:
   - Scroll down to **Tags** and paste the tag comma list from **Section 4**.
   - Ensure **Category** is set to **Science & Technology**.

### Step 5: Video Elements & Visibility
1. Click **Next** through *Video elements* and *Checks*.
2. Under **Visibility**:
   - **Public:** If you want anyone and all competition judges to search and view it immediately.
   - **Unlisted:** If you want only people with the link (e.g. judges reviewing your Devpost submission) to access the video prior to final judging.
3. Click **SAVE** / **PUBLISH**.
4. Copy the resulting YouTube URL (e.g., `https://youtu.be/XXXXXXXXXXX`).
